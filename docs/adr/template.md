## Context

What problem are we solving, and why now? State the forces at play: requirements
from the RFC, constraints, findings from the 0.x audit, and anything we measured.
Keep it factual. A reader who knows nothing about the discussion should understand
why a decision was needed.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in the active voice ("The engine does X", "Stores must Y").
Be concrete: names, defaults, limits and error codes. Add a small TypeScript sketch
when an interface makes the decision clearer than prose.

```ts
// Optional sketch of the public shape this decision fixes.
```

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier or safer.

### Negative

- What gets harder, slower or more expensive, and who pays for it.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

What it is, and why we rejected it.

### Alternative B

What it is, and why we rejected it.
